Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Who we are

Quick Tip Rubbish Solutions is a waste clearance business operating in Brighton and Hove, East Sussex. This policy explains what we do with the personal details you give us through this website. If you have a question about any of it, email info@quicktiprubbish.co.uk.

What we collect

When you fill in the quote form we ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • The area or postcode of the job
  • Details about the job you are asking us to quote for

We do not ask for payment details through this website and we never will. If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email instead, we hold whatever you send us in that message.

Why we use it

We use your details for one thing only: to reply to your enquiry, quote the job and arrange the work. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not send marketing unless you ask us to.

Our legal basis for holding your details is legitimate interest, so that we can respond to an enquiry you chose to send us, and where relevant to carry out a contract with you.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or pass your details to third parties for marketing. The only exception is where we are legally required to share information, for example with the Environment Agency in relation to waste transfer records, or where a payment or accounting record has to be kept.

How it is stored

Form enquiries are delivered to our business email inbox and stored there. We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to quote and complete the job, and to handle any follow up questions afterwards. Waste transfer notes are kept for the period required by law.

Photos of completed work

We photograph jobs before and after, and we sometimes use those photos on this website and on social media. The photos show the waste and the space, not people. If we have photographed your property and you would rather we did not use the image, tell us and we will take it down.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google to fetch them and Google receives your IP address as part of that request.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, or delete it. Email info@quicktiprubbish.co.uk and we will action it within one month.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your details we will update this page and change the date at the top.
